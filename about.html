ABOUT THE PROJECT

Every life has a story.

Where It All Began was created around a simple idea: ordinary people carry extraordinary histories.

Why we exist

Families remember pieces of the past, but details can disappear from one generation to the next.

We want to give people a simple place to tell those stories in their own words.

Our long-term vision is a respectful story archive combined with personal interviews—so people can preserve not only what happened, but how it felt.

OUR PROMISE

Your story belongs to you.

Privacy, consent, and control over what becomes public should be built into the project from the beginning.
